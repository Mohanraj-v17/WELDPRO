Tier-1 Power

Industrial Arc Power Sources

Every trainee station is powered by heavy 3-phase industrial power sources from Lincoln Electric and Miller Electric, delivering stable arc control and digital waveform flexibility.

Miller Dynasty 300 AC/DC TIG: Digital independent amplitude control, squarewave balance, pulse modulation up to 500 PPS.
Lincoln Power Wave S350: Synergic pulse MIG/FCAW with internal Waveform Control Technology for zero-spatter transfer.
Miller Dimension 452 Multi-Process: 450A heavy continuous rating for air carbon-arc gouging and heavy structural flux-cored.
Material Prep

CNC Plasma, Shears & Beveling Tools

Hypertherm CNC Plasma Table

5' x 10' mechanized water table running Powermax 85 torch, cutting up to 1" steel plate with automated torch height control.

Gullco Track Bevellers

Automated plate beveling carriages producing precise 30° and 37.5° AWS bevel angles on structural plate up to 2" thick.

50-Ton Guided Bend Tester

Heavy hydraulic press equipped with AWS and ASME certified bending mandrels (1.5" and 2" radius plungers) for destructive strap evaluations.

Thermal Severing

Heavy Oxy-Fuel & Arcair Gouging Stations

Real jobsites require thermal cutting, root backgouging, and bevel cleanup. Our facility includes Victor Journeyman oxy-acetylene torching stations with mechanized circle burners and motorized pipe bevelers.

For structural weld repair, our Arcair Extreme K4000 gouging torches blast 100 PSI compressed air alongside 400A electric arcs to wash out incomplete root penetration in seconds.

Mechanical Validation

Hydraulic Guided Bend Press

180-Degree Wrap Around

Conforms to AWS D1.1 and ASME Section IX wrap-around test jig specifications.

50-Ton Ram Capacity

Easily bends 3/8" plate and 1" thick side-bend coupons without hydraulic chatter.

Immediate Feedback

Trainees view bend results directly with instructors immediately after joint cooling.

Pipe Fabrication

Precision Cold Cutting & Pipe Beveling Lathes

Clean fit-up is 90% of a successful 6G pipe root pass. Trainees utilize Tri Tool portable split-frame clamshell lathes and heavy Wachs beveling machines to machine exact 37.5° bevels with uniform 1/16" lands on Schedule 40 and 80 carbon pipe.

Eliminating hand-ground variations ensures every practice coupon replicates the strict tolerances of refinery turnaround work.

Metrology Rigor

Calibrated Power Bank Verification

Every machine is checked with certified resistance load banks every 90 days. Voltage, amperage, and high-frequency arc stabilization circuits are tested and labeled with active NIST calibration stickers.

100% Calibrated To AWS B4.0 & ASME Section IX Electrical Standards

Our equipment matches the exact tools used on bridge sites, refineries, and aerospace assembly plants.

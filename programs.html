Six Career-Track Programs

180 Hours • 12 Wks
Heavy Construction

SMAW (Stick) Welding

The bedrock of industrial welding. Master fast-freezing E6010 root runs and low-hydrogen E7018 fills in all positions (1G, 2G, 3G uphill, and 4G overhead).

  • AWS D1.1 Structural Plate Qualification
  • Open-root bevel joint fit-up
  • Visual & 180° guided bend evaluations
Explore SMAW Program
160 Hours • 10 Wks
Manufacturing & Fab

MIG (GMAW) Welding

High-deposition continuous wire welding. Learn short-circuit, globular, axial spray transfer, and pulsed-spray modes for high-volume manufacturing lines.

  • AWS D1.1 / AWS D1.3 Sheet Steel
  • 75/25 Ar/CO2 & 90/10 Tri-Mix gases
  • Synergic pulse parameter setting
Explore MIG Program
220 Hours • 14 Wks
Aerospace & Sanitary

TIG (GTAW) Welding

Surgical precision welding with high-frequency arc starting. Master stainless sanitary tubing, thin-gauge aluminum AC squarewave, and 4130 chromoly tubing.

  • AWS D17.1 Aerospace Fusion
  • Stainless sanitary tube internal back-purging
  • High-frequency AC balance calibration
Explore TIG Program
150 Hours • 10 Wks
Shipyards & Bridges

Flux-Cored (FCAW)

High-deposition flux technology for heavy outdoor bridge erection, ship construction, and seismic columns. Dual-shield gas-assisted and self-shielded wire.

  • AWS D1.1 & AWS D1.5 Bridge Welding Code
  • E71T-1 dual-shield & E71T-8 inner-shield
  • Ceramic backing tape joint setups
Explore FCAW Program
260 Hours • 16 Wks
Refineries & Pipelines

Pipe Welding (5G/6G)

Train in the industry's highest paying position: 6G 45-degree fixed pipe. Learn GTAW root pass with SMAW E7018 fill, plus API 1104 downhill pipeline techniques.

  • ASME Section IX Boiler & Pressure Vessel
  • Schedule 40 & 80 Carbon & Stainless Pipe
  • Side-bend & tensile pull specimens
Explore Pipe Welding

Infrastructure & Towers

Structural Steel Welding

Engineered for commercial ironworkers and steel erectors. Heavy moment frame welding, backing bar fit-up, weld access hole geometry, and ultrasonic inspection standards.

  • AWS D1.1 / AISC 360 Specification
  • Complete joint penetration (CJP) welds
  • Ultrasonic testing (UT) acceptance

Practical Immersion

85% In The Booth • 15% Metallurgical Theory

Trade schools that teach primarily from textbooks produce welders who struggle with arc control. At WELDPRO, your hands are on the torch from week one. We drill puddle oscillation, travel angle, and electrode manipulation through continuous muscle memory repetitions.

Quality Assurance

Guided Bend & Radiographic Testing

Every student weld coupon is subjected to certified non-destructive and destructive testing.

Visual Inspection (VT)

Examination for undercut, porosity, overlap, and uniform reinforcement profile per AWS QC1 code acceptance criteria.

180° Guided Bend Press

Coupons are cut into side-bend and root-bend straps and deformed 180 degrees over our 50-ton hydraulic ram to prove zero internal voids.

X-Ray & Radiography (RT)

6G pressure pipe coupons undergo independent volumetric radiographic inspection to verify complete fusion through the root land.

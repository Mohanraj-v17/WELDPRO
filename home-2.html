01 / Educational Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Welder Mastery

We reject superficial vocational trade shortcuts. WELDPRO trains artisans who understand arc physics, heat-affected zones, and weld puddle chemistry as thoroughly as they handle the torch.

STAGE 01

Metallurgical Foundation

Study iron-carbon equilibrium diagrams, grain growth, carbide precipitation in stainless steels, and hydrogen embrittlement before striking your first arc.

STAGE 02

Motor Memory & Arc Tuning

Hundreds of hours of neuromuscular training. Instructors drill steady travel speeds, electrode angles, and puddle oscillation until keyhole control becomes second nature.

STAGE 03

Destructive Validation

Every completed coupon is visually inspected, saw-cut, and pushed to destruction on our 50-ton hydraulic guided bend press to prove zero internal defects.

02 / High Demand Skills

Mastering The Most Lucrative Industrial Sectors

Industrial fabricators don't just need welders; they need specialists who can read complex isometric drawings, set up pipe purge fixtures, and meet zero-defect tolerances.

Sanitary Stainless & Orbital Tube Welding

Purge-backed food, beverage, and pharmaceutical sanitary tubing conforming to ASME BPE standards.

High-Pressure 6G Boiler & Turbine Tubes

ASME Section IX qualification for steam generation, petrochemical cracking plants, and energy infrastructure.

Heavy Bridge & Seismic Column Moments

AWS D1.1 & D1.5 bridge code compliant multi-pass flux-cored vertical welds with Charpy V-notch toughness.

03 / The Curriculum Architecture

16-Week Rapid Industry Progression

01

Weeks 1–4

Thermal Prep & SMAW

Oxy-fuel & plasma cutting, bevel preparation, joint fit-up, E6010 & E7018 1G–3G plates.

02

Weeks 5–8

MIG & Flux-Cored

Short-circuit, spray pulse, and dual-shield flux-cored on 1/2" and 1" structural steel plate.

03

Weeks 9–12

Precision TIG Mastery

GTAW aluminum AC balance, stainless steel purge tubes, titanium and aerospace root passes.

04

Weeks 13–16

ASME 6G Pipe & Testing

Schedule 80 carbon & stainless pipe in 45° fixed position followed by certified CWI coupon testing.

Built For Scale

A 32,000 Sq Ft Precision Training Facility

At WELDPRO, trainees never wait in line to share an arc. Our facility features 40 private, fully isolated welding booths equipped with dual Miller and Lincoln high-end power sources.

Every station includes independent, high-CFM downdraft and overhead fume extraction hoods ensuring clean, safe breathing air during all-day welding sessions.

Dedicated Lab Wings

Specialized Laboratory Environments

Our campus is divided into three distinct operational bays to replicate specific industry job sites:

Bay 1: Structural & Plate Fabrication

SMAW and FCAW stations with heavy positioning arms for 1G through 4G plate qualification test assemblies.

Bay 2: High-Pressure Pipe & Vessels

Rigid 45-degree pipe fixtures for 2", 6", and 12" Schedule 80 pipe coupons with inert gas backing purge manifolds.

Bay 3: Precision Aerospace Clean Room

Climate-controlled clean bay dedicated to sanitary stainless tube, titanium, and magnesium aircraft components.

OSHA Compliance

Dedicated Safety Training Bay

Industrial safety is not an afterthought—it is a core graduation requirement. Our certified safety bay trains students in OSHA-10 and OSHA-30 general industry standards.

Topics covered include confined space atmospheric testing, hot-work fire watch permits, electrical lockout/tagout (LOTO), and proper PAPR respirator usage.

Shop Culture

A True Job-Site Atmosphere

From mandatory steel-toed boots to daily safety toolbox talks and strict material heat-number tracking, WELDPRO is run like a tier-1 industrial fabrication plant, not a high school shop class.

Strict Shift Punctuality

CWI Daily Inspection

5S Clean Shop Standards

Quality Validation

In-House Destructive & Macro-Etch Laboratory

Our quality verification wing features horizontal band saws, abrasive wet cutoff wheels, chemical macro-etch acid stations, and a 50-ton hydraulic guided bend testing press to inspect internal weld soundess.

50-Ton Bend Ram Guided side and root bend tests per ASME Section IX.
Macro-Etch Station Acid-etching for penetration & HAZ grain inspection.
Immediate coupon cutting & grinding within minutes of arc completion.
CWI-witnessed bend tests simulating real employer hiring qualification exams.
Optical microscopes for examining heat-affected zone (HAZ) microstructures.

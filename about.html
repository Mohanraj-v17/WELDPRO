Who We Are & What We Do

An Industry-Leading Metallurgical & Welding Academy

Unlike broad technical colleges that divide budget across automotive repair, plumbing, and HVAC, WELDPRO is dedicated 100% to metals joining, precision fabrication, and nondestructive testing. Every inch of our facility is engineered to replicate severe commercial job-site conditions.

Each registered trainee is assigned their own private, negative-pressure ventilated booth outfitted with top-tier multi-process inverters from Lincoln Electric and Miller Electric. Students never share booth hours or take turns waiting for arc time.

Our Heritage & Milestone Timeline

Built By Working Boilermakers, Pipefitters & CWI Inspectors

In 2014, a consortium of veteran cross-country pipeline contractors and AWS Certified Welding Inspectors established WELDPRO in response to a crippling shortage: new trade school graduates lacked the hands-on muscle memory to pass radiographic tests on heavy-wall pipe and structural moment joints.

We eliminated general education filler courses, academic lectures that put students to sleep, and simulated plastic torches. From day one, trainees handle live 7018 low-hydrogen electrodes, dual-shield flux-cored guns, and sanitary purge TIG torches. Over 1,800 alumni now earn premier hourly wages on nuclear plants, offshore rigs, and major suspension bridges worldwide.

1 2014: Founded with 12 booths and ASME Section IX pipeline focus.
2 2018: Expanded to 32,000 sq ft and added dedicated CNC Plasma & NDT Lab.
3 2026: Over 1,800 certified graduates placed with AWS D1.1 and ASME qualifications.

Pedagogical Framework

The 85/15 Booth-First Methodology

Welders are forged through torch manipulation, puddle freeze awareness, and joint fit-up—not multiple-choice exams. 85% of every student’s clock hours are spent burning rod, running wire, or feeding filler metal under active arc light.

01

Joint Fit-Up & Bevel Prep

Before striking an arc, students cut, bevel, and grind carbon plate and pipe coupons to exact 37.5° angles with precise root face lands and gap spacing.

Tolerance: ±0.5mm Fitup
02

Neuromuscular Arc Time

Trainees master travel angle, oscillation frequency, arc length, and puddle keyhole management across all positions: 1G, 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, and 6G.

Volume: 250+ Logged Arcs
03

Destructive Bend Tests

After visual CWI approval, students machine their own coupon straps and place them into our hydraulic plunger for an immediate 180° guided side-bend test.

Requirement: Zero Fissures > 1/8"
04

Digital Log Validation

Every signed-off joint, electrode batch number, and test score is timestamped into the trainee's online student portal to build their verified employer portfolio.

Audit: Live CWI Sign-off
Engineering Standards

Trained to International Engineering Codes

A weld certificate is only as valuable as the code it is tested under. WELDPRO students qualify under strict national welding procedure specifications (WPS) that grant full reciprocal qualification across the United States, Canada, and global energy consortia.

AWS D1.1 / D1.5 Structural Steel & Bridge Welding

Multi-pass fillet & complete joint penetration (CJP) groove welds on seismic framing.

ASME Section IX Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code

High-pressure steam pipes, nuclear containment vessels, and 6G fixed coupon testing.

API Standard 1104 Cross-Country Pipelines

High-speed downhill cellulose rod (E6010) root passes and low-hydrogen cap sequences.

AWS D17.1 Aerospace Fusion Specification

High-frequency GTAW on thin-gauge titanium, Inconel, and sanitary stainless steel.

Quick Answers

Admissions & Visiting FAQ

Do I need prior welding experience before touring or applying?

No prior experience is necessary. Our SMAW and MIG fundamentals programs begin from zero, teaching safe booth operation, metallurgy, and joint layout from day one.

Can I bring my own welding hood and PPE for a demo?

Yes! You are welcome to bring your personal hood. Otherwise, WELDPRO provides sanitized Miller auto-darkening hoods and flame-resistant jackets during scheduled hands-on tryout sessions.

When are upcoming cohort start dates published?

New programs begin on the first Monday of every calendar month. Cohorts are strictly capped at 20 trainees to ensure 100% booth availability.

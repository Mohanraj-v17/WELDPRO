At standard trade schools, instructors are often general shop teachers. At WELDPRO, every head instructor holds active American Welding Society Certified Welding Inspector (AWS CWI) or Certified Welding Educator (CWE) credentials.

Mentorship Framework

Daily In-Booth Instruction

At WELDPRO, instructors don't sit behind desks. They wear their masks and stand directly in your booth, observing your torch angle, electrode travel angle, oscillation speed, and puddle width.

Our 4-Step Coaching Loop

  1. Live Torch Demonstration: Instructor lays down the root pass, explaining sound and keyhole visual cues.
  2. Immediate Trainee Practice: Student attempts the identical pass under immediate supervision.
  3. Visual Macro Critique: Joint is examined with magnifier and gauge for uniform throat, leg length, and wash.
  4. Digital Progress Record: Instructor logs feedback into the student dashboard with specific corrective recommendations.
Metallurgical Feedback

Daily Macro-Etch Coupon Sectioning

A weld can look smooth on top while hiding lack of side-wall fusion or incomplete root penetration underneath. Our faculty routinely saws, polishes, and acid-etches student weld coupons to reveal the internal cross-section.

Under nitric acid etching, trainees visually see the heat-affected zone (HAZ), penetration depth into base metal, and individual pass sequence lines.

Stereo zoom optical evaluation up to 40x magnification

Built For High-Precision Craft

Industrial Grade Training Designed For Real-World Fabrication

WELDPRO was founded to bridge the global shortage of high-skilled precision welders. Unlike traditional generalist vocational centers, our 32,000 sq ft facility is engineered exclusively for metallurgical and arc welding craftsmanship.

Students operate individual dedicated booths with independent high-velocity fume extraction, dual Lincoln/Miller power stations, and professional preheat stations. Every weld coupon you strike is subjected to rigorous destructive bend testing and visual inspection according to AWS D1.1 and ASME Section IX codes.

1. Hands-On Trade School

Intensive cohort-based practical shop time with master CWIs holding over 20 years field experience.

1:8 Instructor-To-Student Ratio

No getting lost in giant lecture halls. A dedicated AWS Certified Welding Inspector stands in your booth, correcting your gun angle, arc length, and travel speed in real time.

85% Hands-On Arc Time

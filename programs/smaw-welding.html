The Physics of Shielded Metal Arc Welding

Shielded Metal Arc Welding utilizes a consumable electrode coated in chemical flux. As current flows across the electric arc, the flux burns to create a gaseous shielding cloud that insulates molten weld puddle from atmospheric nitrogen and oxygen.

Because SMAW requires zero external gas shielding bottles, it is the premier choice for outdoor wind-exposed field construction, bridge erection, and rural transmission line maintenance.

E6010 Cellulosic Deep dig, fast-freezing root passes on dirty/rusty steel.
E7018 Low-Hydrogen High tensile strength, crack-resistant structural fills.
Practical Learning Objectives

Core Technical Competencies

All-Position Groove Welds

Master 1G flat, 2G horizontal, 3G vertical-up progression, and 4G overhead plate joints on 3/8" and 1" carbon steel.

Keyhole Control & Tie-Ins

Learn exact whipping and stepping electrode manipulation with E6010 to deposit sound 100% penetration open-root beads without suckback.

Interpass Temperature & Deslag

Maintain AWS preheat standards, use chipping hammers and pneumatic needle scalers to remove slag inclusions between passes.

Booth Hardware

Industrial Heavy Power Stations

SMAW trainees operate heavy constant-current (CC) DC inverter power sources with adjustable arc force and hot start controls to prevent electrode sticking.

  • Miller CST 282 & Lincoln Invertec V275-S
  • Dedicated electrode holding ovens at 250°F for E7018 moisture control
  • Heavy Bernard 300A twist-lock stinger holders with flexible whip leads
Zero Injury Standard

Rigorous SMAW Workshop Safety

Electrical Shock Hazard

Inspect stinger insulation daily; never weld in damp conditions or rest stinger on grounded tables.

Radiation & Arc Eye

Shade 10–12 auto-darkening filter lenses mandatory to protect cornea from UV and IR burn.

Heavy Slag Flying Hazard

Clear safety glasses with side shields worn underneath welding hood during all deslagging.

Job Outcomes

AWS D1.1 Qualification & Placement

Upon completing your 180 hours, you will weld an official AWS D1.1 3G or 4G 1" groove plate coupon. Successful guided bend tests yield your Welder Qualification Test Record (WQTR).

Week-by-Week Breakdown

SMAW Training Progression

Weeks 1–3 • 45 Hrs

Arc Initiation & Flat Fillets

Striking techniques with E6010, bead pad overlaps, 1F and 2F fillet weld profiles, and oxy-fuel bevel prep.

Weeks 4–6 • 45 Hrs

Vertical-Up 3F & 3G

Puddle freeze management in vertical progression, inverted T-joint gussets, and open-root back-gouging.

Weeks 7–9 • 45 Hrs

Overhead 4G & Plate Grooves

Gravity compensation, short arc length maintenance with E7018, backing strip fit-up, and multi-pass weave ties.

Weeks 10–12 • 45 Hrs

AWS D1.1 Qualification Run

1" structural test plate preparation, root pass inspection by CWI, capping, and destructive 180° guided bend testing.

Engineering Standards

Engineered For Continuous Industrial Arc Time

Each 8' x 8' booth features non-reflective welding curtains, heavy 1/2" steel ground plates, adjustable arm positioners, and independent 1,200 CFM source-capture fume extraction arms.

Overhead central manifolds supply 100% Argon, 75/25 Ar/CO2, and 98/2 Ar/O2 shielding gases directly to each station with secondary inline flowmeters for micro-adjustment.

1,200 CFM Source Capture Captures 99.7% of welding particulates at the arc.
Individual Station Lockers Dedicated tool storage for stinger, ground clamp & PPE.
Facility Capabilities

Lab Infrastructure Highlights

Multi-Process Switching

Transition seamlessly between SMAW, GMAW, and GTAW processes with pre-wired patch bays without rewiring secondary leads.

Dedicated Pipe Stands

Heavy structural pipe vices lock 2" through 12" pipe coupons at 45° 6G fixed position with zero movement during root weaving.

Hydraulic Coupon Benders

Test your root and face bend straps on our 50-ton hydraulic ram directly on the shop floor within 5 minutes of joint completion.

Environmental Health

Donaldson Torit Central Extraction Matrix

Welding generates harmful hexavalent chromium and manganese particulate. Our shop runs a continuous 48,000 CFM negative-pressure central filtration collector that purifies and exhausts air with HEPA efficiency.

  • Automated spark arrestor inline baffling
  • Constant ambient air quality monitor display
  • Below 0.005 mg/m³ OSHA PEL compliance

Professional Culture

5S Clean Shop Discipline

Commercial nuclear and aerospace fabricators require absolute tidiness. At WELDPRO, trainees practice daily 5S standards: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, and Sustain.

Zero Trip Hazard Leads hung on elevated swing booms.

Quality Assurance

NIST-Traceable Machine Calibration

To guarantee that a machine set at 125 amps and 22.5 volts delivers true output at the arc, our laboratory equipment is calibrated every quarter using calibrated resistive load banks and certified digital volt-ammeters.

This guarantees that when you practice on WPS qualified settings, your test plates will replicate the identical heat input required by ASME and AWS procedure specifications.

Exotic Metallurgy

Non-Consumable Tungsten & Inert Gas Purity

Gas Tungsten Arc Welding creates an electric arc between an unmelting tungsten electrode and the workpiece. The welder feeds an independent filler rod into the puddle while modulating heat with a foot pedal.

100% pure Argon shielding gas eliminates oxidation, allowing flawless joint integrity in aerospace jet turbine components, titanium bicycle frames, and dairy process piping.

AC Squarewave Cleaning oxide layer on aluminum while controlling penetration depth.
DCEN Pulse Mode Narrow heat-affected zone on thin-wall stainless tubing.
High-Precision Skills

TIG Training Modules

Two-Handed Coordination

Maintain a 1/16" arc gap without dipping tungsten, feeding cold filler wire with one hand while modulating puddle amperage with foot pedal.

Sanitary Tube Purging

Construct internal bladder purge dams to displace oxygen below 10 PPM, producing mirror-smooth internal root passes on 316L dairy tubing.

Aerospace Titanium & Inconel

Use trailing gas shields and argon purge boxes to weld Grade 2 & 5 titanium and Inconel 625 with zero straw or blue discoloration.

Equipment Spec

Each TIG booth is equipped with high-end AC/DC inverters featuring adjustable AC frequency (20–400 Hz), independent EN/EP amperage amplitude, and high-speed pulsing up to 500 PPS.

  • Miller Dynasty 300 with Coolmate 3 water coolers

Safety Standards

High-Frequency Radiation & Inert Gas Handling

Argon Asphyxiation Risk

Argon is heavier than air; booths feature low-level scavenge vents to prevent gas accumulation in pits.

Tungsten Dust Hazards

Dedicated wet dust collectors used during tungsten grinding; rare-earth ceriated/lanthanated electrodes used.

Intense UV Radiation

TIG arcs produce high reflective UV; supple deerskin gloves and tightly buttoned high-neck leathers required.
